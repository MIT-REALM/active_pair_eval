Real KITTI

Failure is the missed-car score on real KITTI: one minus the average overlap between the detector’s boxes and the ground-truth cars. A frame counts as severe when that score is at least 0.45. Coverage is the mean distance between labeled frames in feature space, the same cumulative diversity measure as in the paper. Higher means the labels are spread across more of the scenario space.

How a label gets chosen

One input, two systems. They follow each other, then separate in one place. Each step shortlists the uncovered inputs, then spends the real label where the curves separate.
